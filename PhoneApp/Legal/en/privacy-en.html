Privacy Policy

Your music stays on your devices. This policy explains the data the app handles and how you can manage it.

Version: 2026-10-29Updated and effective: October 29, 2026

No account is required. The current version has no ads, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs, and no developer server that receives your music, playback information, or personal information.

This policy applies to the WatchMusic apps for iPhone and Apple Watch, provided by Li Hao, an independent developer. “The app” below refers to these apps. Your audio and local usage state may contain personal information. Please read this policy and the Terms of Use before using the app.

1. Data handled on your device

Audio and folders you choose to import
The app reads the MP3 and FLAC files you select in the system file picker and stores copies in the app’s storage on your device. It reads file names, sizes, audio duration, modification information, embedded titles, artists, and artwork for playback, display, organization, and verification. It does not scan other files you have not selected.
Playback and preference state
The app stores the play queue, most recent track and position, playback mode, speed, and other necessary state on the current device to restore playback and retain your choices. The system may display current track information on the Lock Screen, headphones, or system playback controls. iPhone and Apple Watch play independently; they do not synchronize or control each other’s playback state.
Transfer and duplicate-checking information
The app stores the pending transfer queue, folder and file names, file sizes, content checksums, task identifiers, states, and necessary timestamps for queuing, duplicate checks, progress display, pausing, and resuming. Checksums are calculated from file contents and are not used for advertising, cross-app identification, or user profiling.
Agreement consent record
After you tap to agree on iPhone, the app stores the accepted agreement version and time locally to determine whether to ask again on a later launch. This record is not uploaded to the developer. Apple Watch does not present a separate consent flow.

This data enables the local features you use. It is not automatically sent to the developer.

2. Transfers between iPhone and Apple Watch

When you choose to send songs, the app uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity to exchange connection information, file manifests, task states, verification information, and save receipts between paired devices. It transfers copies of the selected audio from iPhone to Apple Watch over the same local network. This involves local IP addresses, temporary session identifiers, and access tokens.

The current version transfers audio using local-network HTTP and does not provide TLS transport encryption. The temporary service serves only the files you explicitly select for that transfer. Random access tokens and a session lifetime of up to one hour limit access. SHA-256 verifies file integrity; it does not encrypt audio. Use transfers on a local network you trust.

The iPhone does not write old service credentials to the waiting queue. To restore unfinished tasks, the watch stores a task manifest containing temporary tokens in its local Application Support directory, protected by system file protection and excluded from system backups. Tokens are not written to user-visible transfer records. Old credentials become invalid when a session ends or expires. Transfers are directed to your paired device and do not pass through a developer server.

3. System features used

  • File access: You authorize access to selected audio in the system file picker. If a file comes from iCloud Drive or another file provider, that service and the system handle its download and access.
  • Local network: Used to transfer music on the same Wi-Fi network when the devices can reach each other. You can turn this permission off in system settings. Local playback remains available, but local-network transfers will be affected.
  • Watch battery and motion orientation: The app reads local battery level and charging state, and uses motion orientation to adjust the layout while the app is in the foreground and the watch is charging. This is not used for health analysis or location tracking and is not sent to the developer.
  • Audio and system playback controls: System audio sessions provide playback, audio output selection, and Lock Screen or headphone controls. This is not microphone recording.

The current version does not request location, health, contacts, photo library, or microphone permissions, and does not read the advertising identifier.

4. Third parties, backups, and retention

We do not sell your personal information or provide local music or playback state to advertising or analytics services. This policy, the Terms of Use, and support pages do not set cookies or load analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. Once the website is deployed, its hosting service may retain access logs, such as IP addresses, access times, request paths, and browser information, to serve the website and maintain security. This depends on the host’s configuration and privacy rules. Reading the agreements offline in the app does not require contacting the web host.

When you choose to send a support email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any diagnostic material you voluntarily attach. These are used only to reply, troubleshoot, or handle privacy requests, and are retained for a reasonable period needed to address the matter. You do not need to provide personal details or audio unrelated to the issue. Email providers process messages under their own rules. The app does not automatically send email in the background.

When you choose to visit the support page on GitHub, GitHub may process your IP address, browser information, account, and submitted content under its own privacy rules. This is not background uploading by the app.

Music and some local app state may be included in your device’s iCloud or computer backups. Whether data is backed up, where it is stored, and how long it is retained depend on the system and relevant service settings. We do not provide a separate developer-operated cloud synchronization or cloud deletion service.

Imported music and usage state generally remain on the corresponding device until you delete or clear them or remove the app. Temporary files are cleaned up during task processing. Clearing data on one device does not automatically delete copies on the other device, original audio, or existing system backups.

5. Your choices and data management

  • You can view, move, or delete local folders and audio in each device’s library.
  • Clearing transfer history removes only tasks or records. It does not delete songs already saved on iPhone or Apple Watch.
  • You can manage local-network permissions, file providers, and device backups in system settings. You can also remove the app and manage its data in system backups.
  • If you do not agree, cancel the initial prompt without entering the iPhone library or transfer features. If you later withdraw agreement, use Settings → Privacy Policy on iPhone to withdraw consent and stop using the app on that phone. You can also stop using the app and delete the relevant local app data. Withdrawal itself does not automatically delete music on either device, original files, or system backups. Contact us if you need further help.
  • The app has no accounts, so there is no separate developer-account deletion process. You can privately request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights through the support email below. For data stored only on your devices, we cannot remotely view or delete it for you, but can explain the relevant local steps.

Deleting a file on iPhone does not delete it on Apple Watch, and vice versa. To remove it completely, manage both devices and any backups you retain separately.

6. Use by minors

Minors should read this policy and use the app with guidance from a guardian. Obtain guardian consent first where applicable law requires it. We do not intentionally collect information from minors through the app. Contact us with any questions about related data.

7. Policy updates

When features or data handling change, we will update this policy and show its version and date. Changes that require renewed consent will be presented in the app. You can review this policy and the Terms of Use at any time under Transfers → Settings → Privacy Policy on iPhone.
