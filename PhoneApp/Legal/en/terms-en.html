1. Features

The app provides local playback and folder management for MP3 and FLAC audio you import, and local-network file transfers from iPhone to a paired Apple Watch. Each device manages its own audio copies and plays independently. The app does not provide an online music catalog, music purchases, content downloads, or cross-device playback control.

Local-network transfers require both devices to reach each other on the same Wi-Fi network, with both apps active in the foreground. Leaving the app, locking the screen, system restrictions, or connection changes may pause tasks. When you return, the app attempts to check the connection and resume; manually paused tasks remain paused. An unfinished song may need to be sent again. Speeds and remaining times shown in the interface are estimates, not guaranteed completion times.

Actual features depend on device model, operating system version, audio encoding, storage space, and system behavior. Automatic watch rotation, battery display, audio outputs, and screen behavior depend on device and system support.

4. File management and backups

Importing creates audio copies within the app. Later deleting or moving a local copy does not automatically change the original file. File management on iPhone and Apple Watch is independent. Clearing transfer history does not delete music already saved.

Keep original files or separate backups of important audio. The scope and results of system backups depend on your system settings and services and are not guaranteed by the app.

Do not assume a file has been saved merely because transfer progress reaches 100%. Rely on the success state returned after the watch verifies and saves the file.
