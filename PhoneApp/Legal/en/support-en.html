Get started

  1. Create a folder firstIn Library on iPhone, tap the new-folder button in the upper-right corner. Open the folder and use the import button to select MP3 or FLAC files. You can choose local files, iCloud Drive files, or files from other providers in the system Files picker.
  2. Send music to your watchConnect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network so they can reach each other, allow local-network access on iPhone, and open both apps. Choose Send to Watch in the song or folder actions.
  3. Wait for saving to finishIn Transfers, check the Active and Completed lists. Songs queue one at a time, and you can add songs from other folders while transfers are running. Confirm completion with the Saved to Watch status.
  4. Play independently on either deviceOnce saved, music can play offline on your watch without a continuous phone connection. Both devices play independently; neither pauses the other, changes its tracks, or synchronizes playback state.

Transfers

Why is a transfer paused or failed, or asking to retry after I return?

Transfers run only while both apps are active in the foreground. Leaving the app, locking the screen, opening Control Center, or returning to the watch face may automatically pause tasks. Reopen both apps to let them check the connection and try to resume. For manually paused tasks, tap Start.

If the connection is still unavailable, check that the devices can reach each other over Wi-Fi, local-network permission is on for iPhone, and the watch has enough battery and storage. Some guest Wi-Fi networks isolate devices; try another network you trust. An unfinished song may be sent again after resuming.

Can the phone keep transferring in the background?

The current version uses foreground local-network transfers and does not support reliable continued background transfer. Keep both iPhone and Apple Watch in the app during transfers. The iPhone temporarily keeps its screen awake while transferring in the foreground and restores the setting afterward. watchOS controls brightness, screen sleep, and execution state. Charging does not mean background transfers are permanently allowed.

Is it normal for transfers to be slow or the remaining time to change?

Speed depends on Wi-Fi, device state, file size, and system scheduling. Verifying and saving large files and waiting for receipts also take time. Remaining time is a rough estimate based on recent speed and may be hidden when paused or speed is unknown. Even after transfer progress reaches 100%, you must wait for the watch to verify and save the file.

Keep both devices near the network access point and sufficiently charged. You can use the watch while charging; the app automatically adjusts its display for the charging orientation.

Can I send multiple folders? Will songs already sent be duplicated?

You can add songs from other folders during transfers. All tasks queue one song at a time in the order added. Before sending, the app checks the actual watch files’ folders, names, sizes, and content checksums. Files with identical contents at the same path are skipped; clearing phone history does not affect this check. A file may be sent again if it has been deleted from the watch, has a different path, or its content has changed.

Audio and library

Why can’t a file be imported or played?

MP3 and FLAC are currently supported. Check that the file has finished downloading from its provider, is not empty, has no DRM access restrictions, and can be decoded by the system. Renaming a file extension does not convert its audio format. Make sure both iPhone and Apple Watch have enough storage, then retry with the original file.

How do I select, move, or delete multiple songs?

Open a folder and touch and hold a song to enter selection mode. Select songs, then tap Actions in the upper-right corner to send, move, or delete them. Tap Done to finish selecting. The Library home page lists only folders and does not offer song selection.

Why don’t the phone and watch control each other’s playback?

They are independent players. Previous Track, Play or Pause, and Next Track affect only the current device. Use the corresponding device’s system output picker to choose an available audio output. Pairing and output capabilities depend on the system and device.

Why does the watch rotate while charging, and how do I adjust volume?

While charging with the app in the foreground, the interface automatically rotates to match a stable placement orientation. When not charging, it returns to the portrait orientation used on your wrist. If placed flat or orientation data is unavailable, the most recent stable orientation is retained. In the player, use the Digital Crown to adjust local system volume. The system determines which audio outputs are available.

History and data

Does clearing transfer history delete songs?

No. The cleanup button in the upper-right corner clears tasks or records in the current Transfers tab, canceling associated unfinished tasks first. Music already saved on iPhone or Apple Watch is kept.

How do I delete my music and app data?

Delete local files or folders in the corresponding device’s library. The phone and watch have separate copies, which must be deleted separately. Manage original files and system backups in their respective locations. You can also remove the app and manage device backups in system settings. See the Privacy Policy for more details.

Where can I find the agreements and app version?

Open Settings in the upper-right corner of Transfers on iPhone, to the left of the cleanup button. Privacy Policy provides the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use and lets you withdraw consent. About shows the app name and version. Apple Watch does not present a separate consent flow.
