## Description

```text
Your music, on your wrist.

WatchMusic is a local audio player for iPhone and Apple Watch. Import your own MP3 and FLAC files, organize them into folders, and send the tracks you want to your watch. Once saved, your Apple Watch can play them offline without an ongoing iPhone connection.

Start with your own files
• Import MP3 and FLAC through the system Files picker.
• See embedded cover art, titles and artists.
• Organize folders and long-press tracks to send, move or delete several at once.

Send music over Wi-Fi
• Queue tracks from multiple folders in one sequential transfer queue.
• See transfer progress, speed and an approximate remaining time.
• Skip matching tracks already saved on the watch.
• Pause or continue tasks. Leaving either app pauses transfer; reopening both apps checks the connection and attempts to continue.

Listen on Apple Watch
• Previous, play or pause, and next controls in a compact player.
• Sequential, shuffle and repeat playback modes.
• Playback speed controls and a sleep timer.
• System audio output and volume controls.
• Battery status and an app layout that adapts to the watch's placement while charging.

Listen on iPhone too
The iPhone player works independently, with system playback controls, a compact library playback bar, and light and dark appearances.

Made for your own music
No account is required. The current version has no ads or behavioral tracking, and does not upload music or listening history to a developer server.

Before transferring
Use a paired iPhone and Apple Watch on the same reachable Wi-Fi network and keep both apps open during transfer. An incomplete track may restart when a transfer is retried. Once a track is saved on the watch, offline playback does not need a network connection. Each device plays independently.

WatchMusic plays audio that you import and have permission to use. It does not provide a streaming catalog or music-download service. The current app interface is in Simplified Chinese.

Requires iOS 17 or later and watchOS 10 or later. Audio output and some system features depend on device and operating system support.

Support: 2114581274@qq.com
Developer: Li Hao
```
