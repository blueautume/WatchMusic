FROM YOUR FILES TO YOUR WRIST

三步，把喜欢的音乐带走。Three steps to take your music along.

01 / IMPORT

建立目录，导入音乐Create a folder. Add your music.

在 iPhone 音乐库新建目录，进入后点右上角加号，从系统“文件”选择自己的 MP3 或 FLAC。Create a folder in the iPhone library. Open it, tap the plus button, and select your own MP3 or FLAC files with the system file picker.

02 / TRANSFER

两端打开，局域网传输Open both apps. Transfer over Wi-Fi.

让 iPhone 与已配对的 Apple Watch 处于可互通的 Wi-Fi 网络。选好歌曲发送，已保存且内容相同的歌曲会跳过。Keep your iPhone and paired Apple Watch on a Wi-Fi network that allows them to reach each other. Send your selection; matching songs already saved on the watch are skipped.

03 / LISTEN

传好之后，手表独立播放Once transferred, listen on the watch.

在手表中打开目录，选择歌曲。音乐已在腕上，之后播放无需手机，也无需网络。Open a folder on your watch and choose a song. The music is already there, ready to play without your iPhone or a network connection.

传输期间请保持手机和手表 App 打开。离开 App 会自动暂停，回来后重试；大批量传输建议给手表充电。播放已传输的音乐不受此要求影响。Keep both apps open during transfer. Leaving an app pauses transfer; returning retries it. Charging your watch is recommended for larger queues. These requirements do not apply to playback of songs already on the watch.

GOOD TO KNOW

开始之前，
你可能想知道。A few things
before you begin.

随身音乐提供在线歌曲吗？Does WatchMusic include an online music catalog?

随身音乐用于管理和播放你自行导入的音频文件，不提供在线曲库、流媒体服务或音乐下载。请确保你有权使用所导入的内容。WatchMusic organizes and plays audio files that you import yourself. It does not include a streaming catalog or music downloads. Please make sure you have the right to use the files you import.

播放时必须带着 iPhone 吗？Do I need my iPhone with me to listen?

音乐传到手表后，Apple Watch 可以独立离线播放。iPhone 用于导入、整理和传输；手机与手表各自独立播放。No. Once songs are stored on Apple Watch, it can play them offline on its own. Use iPhone to import, organize, and transfer. The two devices have independent playback.

传输时能把手机锁屏或切到后台吗？Can I lock my iPhone or leave the app during transfer?

当前版本采用前台局域网传输。请保持两端 App 打开，离开时会暂停，返回后重试。未完成的单首歌曲可能需要重新传输。The current version transfers while both apps are open in the foreground. Leaving pauses the transfer; returning retries it. An unfinished song may need to be sent again from the beginning.

为什么有时局域网连接不成功？Why might the local network connection fail?

请确认两端可连接到同一网络，并已允许本地网络权限。部分访客或公共 Wi-Fi 会隔离设备，不适合局域网传输。详细步骤可查看技术支持。Check that both devices can reach the same network and that local network permission is enabled. Some guest or public Wi-Fi networks isolate devices and may prevent transfers. See support for help.

支持哪些设备和文件？Which devices and files are supported?

需要 iOS 17 或更新版本的 iPhone，以及 watchOS 10 或更新版本的 Apple Watch。支持导入 MP3 和 FLAC，实际播放兼容性取决于文件编码与设备系统。WatchMusic requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later and an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. It imports MP3 and FLAC files; playback compatibility depends on file encoding and the device system.
